# Repository instructions

- Do not use em or en dashes in authored UI copy, documentation, comments, or generated text. Use ordinary punctuation and ASCII hyphens for ranges. Do not rewrite third-party legal text, applied migrations, or historical records to enforce this rule.
- When replacing functionality, remove confirmed unused packages, fonts, imports, assets, and obsolete license notices. Preserve required dependencies and resources whose use has not been ruled out.
- Keep fonts FOSS and retain the licenses for every font still used.
- Display complete dates as yyyy/dd/mm with zero-padded day and month. For example, 1 October 2026 is 2026/01/10. Use Tokyo time for timestamps and preserve the calendar day of date-only records. Keep ISO formats for database/API values, machine-readable HTML attributes, and native date-input values.
